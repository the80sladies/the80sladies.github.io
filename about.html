About The Club

What It Is

A neon love letter to 80s ladies everywhere.

The 80s Ladies Club is a retro nostalgia community celebrating the fashion, music, hairstyles, humor, malls, electronics, and everyday memories of the 1980s.

It is built around the kind of details people remember instantly: hairspray clouds in the bathroom, giant earrings, shoulder pads, phone cords, cassette tapes, mall trips, boomboxes, VHS rentals, arcade nights, big attitudes, and outfits that refused to be quiet.

Every video, blog post, image, and design is created to bring those memories back with humor, color, nostalgia, and enough hairspray to hold the decade together.

Why It Exists

Because the 80s had a feeling.

The 1980s were loud, funny, colorful, dramatic, and completely unforgettable. The hair was bigger, the music was brighter, the malls felt like worlds of their own, and even ordinary moments had personality.

The 80s Ladies Club exists to bring that feeling back in a modern way through short videos, funny throwback moments, community posts, retro-inspired products, and a growing online home for anyone who loves the decade.

Whether you grew up in the 80s or simply wish you had, this club is built for people who still love the style, energy, music, humor, and memories of 1980s culture.

The Mission

Keep the nostalgia alive.

The mission is simple: keep the spirit of the 1980s alive through fun, creativity, and nostalgia.

The 80s Ladies Club is here to make people smile, spark memories, celebrate 80s style, and build a fun retro community around the women, fashion, music, products, and everyday chaos of the decade.

The direction is clear: more videos, more community moments, more retro products, more stories, more blog articles, and more reasons to say, “I remember that.”
