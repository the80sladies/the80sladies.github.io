What It Is

A neon love letter to 80s ladies everywhere.

The 80s Ladies Club is a retro nostalgia world celebrating the fashion, music, hairstyles, humor, malls, electronics, and everyday memories of the 1980s.

It is built around the kind of details people remember instantly: hairspray clouds in the bathroom, giant earrings, shoulder pads, phone cords, cassette tapes, mall trips, boomboxes, big attitudes, and outfits that refused to be quiet.

Social media is where the daily fun happens. Etsy is where the retro gifts live. This website is the official clubhouse that brings the whole neon universe together.

Why It Exists

Because the 80s had a feeling.

The 80s were loud, funny, colorful, dramatic, and completely unforgettable. The hair was bigger, the music was brighter, the malls felt like worlds of their own, and even ordinary moments had personality.

The 80s Ladies Club exists to bring that feeling back in a modern way — through short videos, funny throwback moments, community posts, retro-inspired products, and a growing online home for anyone who loves the decade.

The Mission

Keep the nostalgia alive.

The mission is simple: make people smile, spark memories, celebrate 80s style, and build a fun retro community around the women, fashion, music, products, and everyday chaos of the decade.

The 80s Ladies Club is still growing, but the direction is clear: more videos, more community moments, more retro products, more stories, and more reasons to say, “I remember that.”
